Agreement for Legal Services

This Agreement is made between the Client, a company named in Annex 1, and the Firm, a law firm named in Annex 1, each a Party and together the Parties, on the date the last Party signs it.

  1. Subject
    1. The Firm shall advise the Client on matters of commercial and contract law that the Client refers to it in writing, and shall represent the Client in negotiations where the Client so asks.
    2. A matter is referred when the Client sends a request describing it and the Firm accepts it in writing within three working days.
  2. Fees
    1. The Client shall pay for the hours the Firm spends on a matter at the rates set out in Annex 2.
    2. The Firm shall send an invoice at the end of each month, with the hours spent on each matter and a short account of the work.
    3. An invoice is due within fourteen days of its receipt. Interest on late payment accrues at the rate set by law.
  3. Confidentiality
    1. Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.
    2. This obligation survives the end of this Agreement for five years.
  4. Liability
    1. The Firm is liable for loss caused to the Client by advice given in breach of the care expected of a law firm.
    2. The liability of the Firm for a matter is limited to the fees paid for that matter in the twelve months before the loss arose, except for loss caused intentionally or by gross negligence.
  5. Term and termination
    1. This Agreement is made for an indefinite term.
    2. Either Party may terminate it by notice in writing with one month's notice period. Matters already referred shall be completed unless the Client decides otherwise.
  6. Final provisions
    1. This Agreement is governed by the law of the country in which the Firm has its seat. Disputes shall be settled by its courts.
    2. Changes to this Agreement are valid only in writing and signed by both Parties.

Signed by the Parties:

for the Client ______________________    for the Firm ______________________
