# plyn.prastora

A place of Plyń. Nothing about it is copied here; each thing is kept in one place, and this file says where.

- What this place is, where its work goes, how it is checked when it has a way of its own, and what each of its files is: its readings, `pračytańnie/en.toml` (apisańnie.uvachod, apisańnie.kirunak, apisańnie.pravierka, samaadrasavańnie).
- Its constituents, the answer it gives each, and the norms of its own: its registry, `miesca.toml`.
- Its work: its journal, `śled.toml`, read with the tool journal_view of the frame's Klučnik, this place as the root.
- How every agent works in every place of Plyń, of every owner (whom a text is for, where data goes, reaching GitHub, checking and landing, the journal) and the norms of the system: plyn-one/plyn.mova, `docs/praca.md` and `rejestr.toml`, read with the tool github_read of the frame's Klučnik before work.
- Open questions of the system: plyn-one/plyn.laba.
