A page that keeps its composition on every screen

Within a step of the ladder the page scales as a whole, like a picture: nothing moves relative to anything. Between steps the relations fold by themselves. Body text never falls below the reader's own size, and zoom makes it larger.

Three parts

  • The core

    One stylesheet: the ladder of steps, tokens, plain HTML styled, and eight relations.

    ready

  • The manifest

    The whole vocabulary on one page, small enough to stay in an agent's context.

    ready

  • The check

    Renders the page at both edges of every step and says what moved or broke.

    ready

  • Themes

    Values only: a theme changes colours and type, never the composition.
